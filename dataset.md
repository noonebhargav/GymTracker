# Exercise Dataset Taxonomy

This document outlines the taxonomy used in the `hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset`. It breaks down how exercises are categorized anatomically.

## Body Parts (10 total)
The broadest categorization, usually representing a general region of the body.

- `back`
- `cardio`
- `chest`
- `lower arms` (Forearms)
- `lower legs` (Calves)
- `neck`
- `shoulders`
- `upper arms` (Biceps & Triceps)
- `upper legs` (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes)
- `waist` (Abs & Core)

## Targets (19 total)
The specific primary muscle or system targeted by the exercise.

- `abductors` (Outer thigh)
- `abs` (Waist)
- `adductors` (Inner thigh)
- `biceps` (Upper arms)
- `calves` (Lower legs)
- `cardiovascular system` (Cardio)
- `delts` (Shoulders)
- `forearms` (Lower arms)
- `glutes` (Upper legs)
- `hamstrings` (Upper legs)
- `lats` (Back)
- `levator scapulae` (Neck/Upper back)
- `pectorals` (Chest)
- `quads` (Upper legs)
- `serratus anterior` (Chest/Ribs)
- `spine` (Back)
- `traps` (Back/Shoulders)
- `triceps` (Upper arms)
- `upper back` (Back)
